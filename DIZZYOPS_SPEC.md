# DizzyOps | AfrodizzyActs

Temporary development home: burnt-exe/courseware.co.za. Final intended destination: Afrodizyactz/DizzyOps.

## Design contract
Match the original AfrodizzyActs website branding, logo, typography, colors, spacing, imagery and interaction style. Do not treat black/red/white alone as sufficient proof of visual conformance. Responsive desktop and mobile navigation, active route indicators, breadcrumbs, keyboard focus and WCAG AA contrast are required.

## Navigation contract (15 distinct pages)

1. /dashboard — KPIs, events, alerts
2. /performers — profiles, onboarding, skills, availability, documents
3. /contractors — supplier records, agreements, rates, compliance
4. /bookings — enquiries, quotes, assignments, jobs
5. /calendar — events, availability, conflict detection
6. /projects — budgets, timelines, milestones, teams
7. /finance — cash flow, approvals, profitability
8. /expenses — receipts, claims, allocation, approvals
9. /invoices — invoices, balances, payments
10. /assets — documents, costumes, props, inventory
11. /reports — analytics, utilization, trends
12. /integrations/sage — connection status and mapping
13. /integrations/google — Workspace connection status and settings
14. /settings — users, roles, permissions
15. /portal — performer self-service

Each route needs its own view, appropriate actions, consistent navigation and empty/error states. Never imply backend, authentication, Sage or Google integrations are functional until verified.
